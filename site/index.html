Wi-Fi USB Stick

Built for changing a Tesla's custom lock chime from your phone. A LilyGo T-Dongle S3 (with or without the screen) acts as the car's USB drive: join its Wi-Fi, listen to your .wav files, tap Make Lock Chime, done. It also works as a general Wi-Fi USB drive: upload, rename and delete files from your phone or computer.

Only for the LilyGo T-Dongle S3 (the version with the LCD screen, or the one without). Other boards will not work, and flashing them may leave them needing a manual recovery.

2. Install

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop. Firefox, Safari and phones can't flash from a web page.
  2. Unplug the dongle. Press and hold the button on it, then plug it in while still holding it. Release after it is plugged in.
  3. Click Install below and choose the entry that looks like USB JTAG/serial debug unit (or "ESP32-S3") in the pop-up list.
  4. Wait for it to finish, then unplug and re-plug the dongle without holding the button.
Your browser can't flash devices. Please open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Flashing needs a secure (https) page.

Installing replaces the dongle's firmware and, if you tick "Erase device", its saved Wi-Fi settings. Files on the microSD card are not touched.

After installing

  1. Insert a microSD card formatted as FAT32.
  2. Plug the dongle in. It shows up as a USB drive on the computer, car or printer it is plugged into.
  3. On your phone or laptop, join the Wi-Fi network you chose above, then open http://192.168.4.1.
  4. Use the page to switch to Wi-Fi mode, upload or rename files, or join your home Wi-Fi.
